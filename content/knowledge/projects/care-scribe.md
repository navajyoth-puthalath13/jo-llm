- CARE is a hospital information system developed by Open Healthcare Network.
- CARE Scribe helps turn doctor–patient conversations into clinical notes.
- My case study explores how conversation capture could follow clinicians when they move away from their workstation.
- My role: as part of my open-source design contribution to Open Healthcare Network, I explored this direction independently and proposed it to the CARE team. It was an initiative I took on outside my assigned work.
- The team was already considering a wearable microphone. I looked at its practical constraints and explored using a smartphone as a capture companion.
- What I explored: how doctors move between the desk and other parts of a consultation, a wearable microphone direction, and a phone as a capture companion.
- The direction I proposed: mobile for conversation capture, with CARE Desktop staying the place to review and complete documentation.
- What I'm proud of: I tried to understand the workflow and practical constraints before settling on a direction, including setup, maintenance, connectivity and how a solution could fit into the way clinicians already work.
- Status: a design direction I proposed and discussed with the team. It has not launched, shipped or been adopted. Never describe it as a launched CARE feature.
