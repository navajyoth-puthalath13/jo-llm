- The case study's interactive frames mostly play themselves with a small cursor; pausing them, or clicking inside, lets the visitor try them. The CARE screens use CARE's own design tokens and the Figtree font. All doctors and patients are invented names.
- Cover and work card: CARE's doctor availability screen for an invented doctor, "Dr. Anaya Menon" (General Medicine, February 2026 calendar), dimmed, with the redesigned Create Schedule Template sheet open and filled in ("Regular OP Day", Feb 10 to Mar 10 2026, Mon to Fri, Private, "Morning Consultations" 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM, Manual, 15-minute slots). On a computer, hovering the card makes the form fill itself in again; on phones it loops. The background is a muted sage green (#DCE8DF), chosen to frame the white interface and echo CARE's green, with no laptop frame.
- "Inside the CARE ecosystem": a map with the CARE logo in the middle and its modules around it (patient records, clinical encounters, documentation, orders, labs and pharmacy, tasks and care plans, referrals, billing and reporting, integrations). "Scheduling & appointments" is highlighted. Tagline: "Many moving parts. One connected system."
- "Inside CARE: Appointment template creation": a screenshot of the old form.
- The "Current workflow / Intended direction" illustration: a tangled, looping line passes a green dot and becomes three straight parallel lines. It means a tangled setup process made clear.
- The checklist illustration: a line drawing of me holding a big checklist with two items ticked, between "Appointment experience" and "Scheduling logic", the two things the work improved.
- "The existing appointment-template experience": the old form, live, playing a case until CARE rejects it (today refused as a start date, then the 14.9-minute fractional slot, then "must be a multiple of slot size"). It shows the errors on purpose, as the "before" state, so visitors can feel the problem.
- The three friction tiles: the old weekday buttons, auto-fill switch and public checkbox taking turns; a zoom into an old session ending in the fractional-slot pop-up; and pop-ups dropping in at the top, away from the fields.
- Exploration tiles: the redesigned pieces (presets, Private or Public, Manual or Auto with live totals); the three slot-logic iterations, each shown first as an animated timeline ("logic") and then as a working form ("interface"), switchable with "Show interface" or "Show logic", with the third tagged "Engineering sync"; and inline errors appearing under the field.
- Decision tiles: before and after cards for weekly schedule, booking visibility and slot configuration; then the old 149-minute fractional slot failing beside the factor-based options, driven by one shared control.
- Solution tile: the full redesigned form plays itself, with two sessions, one date mistake caught inline under Valid Till, then a successful save. Visitors can use it afterwards.
- Reflection: two points side by side, "Using AI to understand the codebase" and "Working through real scheduling constraints". Then "Back to top" and the tags.
- Moving around: on a computer, a left menu lists the chapters, with the current one in green; on phones, a ruler-like pill at the bottom works like a zoom dial: drag to a section and let go. The old address /work/new-case-study still forwards here.
