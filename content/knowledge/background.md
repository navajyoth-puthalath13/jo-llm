- I started studying engineering in 2021.
- I enjoyed building things, but the projects I worked on made me think beyond whether something was technically possible.
- A product can be impressive to build and still not become something people want or know how to use.
- I realised getting something to work technically was only part of the challenge. People also need to understand it, find it useful and have a good experience using it.
- I began exploring UX and product design outside college, learning from design communities and meeting people who thought about products from a different angle.
- I didn't leave engineering behind. I brought that understanding into product design.
- Understanding implementation helps me ask better questions, work closely with engineers and make design decisions with real constraints in mind.
