- When I'm not making something, I'm probably lost in Minecraft, building my own little world.
- What I'm learning right now: exploring AI, building with new tools, and figuring out what product design looks like when interfaces can generate themselves. I learn from my projects, the people I meet and the things I notice every day.
- Advice for people starting out: stay curious, make things and learn by trying. You don't need to have everything figured out before you begin. You can also message me on LinkedIn.
